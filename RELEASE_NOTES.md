# NexusWealth Release Notes

## [v3.1.3]
- **Streamlined Transaction Entry**: Simplified the transaction creation modal by removing the repeat setting tab, keeping manual expense and income logging clean and clutter-free (recurring transactions continue to be managed seamlessly from Settings).
- **Clean Settings & Security Layout**: Removed the duplicate admin button at the bottom of the Settings screen, providing a single consolidated Admin Portal card for administrators.

---

## [v3.1.2]
- **Timezone-Aware Recurring Execution**: Recurring transactions now execute precisely according to your local timezone (e.g. Malaysia Time / Asia/Kuala_Lumpur) instead of remote server time.
- **Accurate Local Time Scheduling**: Execution times and creation defaults automatically match the user's local clock across daily, weekly, and monthly recurrence.
- **Timezone Labeling**: View your active schedule timezone directly within recurring settings and transaction forms.

---

## [v3.1.1]
- **Custom Execution Time for Recurring Transactions**: Choose the exact time of day your recurring transactions execute, or let it default automatically to creation time.
- **Streamlined Settings Navigation**: Consolidated recurring and repeat settings into a single, clean Recurring Transactions management screen.
- **Execution Time Indicators**: View and edit scheduled execution times directly on your recurring schedule cards and in transaction forms.

---

## [v3.1.0]
- **Automated Recurring Transactions**: Schedule repeated daily, weekly, or monthly expenses, incomes, or transfers automatically with dedicated per-user automated cron scheduling.
- **Single-Job Protection & Control**: Manage your active recurring schedule directly from Settings with pause, resume, instant execution, and automatic balance updates.
- **Repeat Preferences in Transactions**: Create recurring transactions directly while logging expenses with seamless defaults and schedule replacement guards.
- **Recurring Activity Indicators**: Easily spot automated transactions in your transaction history with dedicated recurrence badges.

---

## [v3.0.6]
- **Multi-Currency Account Transfers**: Added real-time currency conversion between accounts in different currencies with customizable exchange rates and live preview.
- **Accurate Balance Accounting**: Debited and credited amounts are tracked natively per account currency to prevent balance mismatches.
- **App Updates & Changelog**: In-app updates now display dedicated, clean release notes instead of repeating prior commits.
- **Performance & Security**: Optimizations for transaction sync and enhanced stability.

---

## [v3.0.5]
- **Transaction History Enhancement**: Improved account transaction view and balance breakdowns.
- **Offline Sync Stability**: Resolved edge cases in offline queue management and retry logic.
